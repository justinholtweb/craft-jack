Art. 13 DSGVO

Google Fonts is loaded from Google

Munich Regional Court I awarded damages against a site for embedding Google Fonts dynamically without consent (20 January 2022, 3 O 17493/20), and a cottage industry of warning letters followed.

Fix: Download the fonts and serve them from your own domain, then remove the entry from the inventory.
